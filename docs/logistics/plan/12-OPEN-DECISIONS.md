# Logistics Decision Register

## Decision rules

An approved decision records accountable owner, evidence, approved rule, effective date/version, affected scope, superseded rule and review trigger. A meeting, recommendation, current code path or test fixture is not approval. Open mandatory decisions block only affected phases; Q1-Q10 and applicable Q13 must close before the all-mode pilot. Q11/Q12 are deferred because approved manual/internal paths exist.

## Q3 approved stock-balance identity

The registered Inventory migration proposes one balance row for each distinct combination of `company_id`, `product_id`, `location_id`, `lot_id`, `package_id` and `partner_id`. These mean the legal Company, stock item, physical Inventory location, tracked production lot, physical package and stock owner, respectively. A missing lot, package or stock owner is a real empty value: two rows with the same remaining fields and the same empty value are duplicates. Quantities and reservations belong to that one row; neither counted quantity, storage category nor a delivery customer creates a second stock balance. Product quantities use the approved Inventory unit, not a customer-facing pack unit.

### A. Quantity units, packaging and remaining demand

Use each product's approved Inventory stock unit for stock arithmetic. Keep the customer's quantity and packaging separately, with the conversion and precision that applied when Sales confirmed the commitment. Record actual loaded quantities and packaging in a separate load revision. A later packaging-master edit must not reinterpret either historical record.

Require a known, positive conversion in the correct unit category. For example, six packs containing twelve stock units represent seventy-two stock units, not seventy-two units plus six additional packs. Reject quantities that cannot be represented at the approved precision rather than silently rounding a shipment. A sealed pack is a whole pack; an opened pack or fractional stock quantity is allowed only where the product's approved handling and unit rules support it. A variable-weight pack must not use an invented fixed conversion. Missing mappings hold the affected load rather than disabling unrelated products or planning work.

Plan the remaining quantity of eligible confirmed Sales lines within an explicit loading window. An authorized planner may include, defer or split quantities with a reason; tentative planning does not require a confirmed vehicle. Keep the undelivered remainder open by default. Cancelling that remainder requires an explicit Sales decision. Partial loading requires an approved commercial quantity or partial-delivery arrangement, preserves the remaining demand and native backorder links, and never marks the entire order delivered.

### B. Available stock, production and loading checks

Use the four assessment results and separate quantities already specified in [F1](03-WORKFLOWS-AND-LIFECYCLES.md#stock-readiness-before-loading-proposed-target-for-review): ready from current stock, forecast dependent, shortfall and unresolved inputs. A positive assessment is information, not a reservation or permission to depart. Only eligible stock recorded by Inventory can be reserved or issued. Forecast production and planned inbound transfers remain future supply until the native receipt and required quality release occur. A production ready-at estimate includes required packing, cooling and quality checks.

Record the actual loading origin, product, quantity, lot and package information required for the goods. Verify the load against the current Sales commitment, reservation, vehicle and applicable handling rules. Required capacity measurements include packaging and pallets where relevant. Check each segment using cargo already aboard plus newly loaded cargo, and apply the vehicle's approved limits. Missing required measurements or quality decisions hold the affected release; a known safety prohibition cannot be overridden. Numeric tolerances, capacity values and product restrictions are explicit master data approved by the responsible owner, not arbitrary software defaults.

After a material change to goods, quantity, packaging, lot, vehicle, destination or loading origin, create a new load revision and repeat affected checks and approvals. After stock issue, changing a field cannot relocate or restore goods: use the linked Inventory correction or return workflow. An onsite abort preserves the original issue and records the compensating native operation. Returned quantities are bounded by the original issued quantity less earlier effective returns; a physical receipt and the required condition or quality assessment are separate facts.

### D. Normal responsibilities and approval separation

The planner selects demand and proposes shortage recovery. Production confirms its expected output and ready-at time. The warehouse records the load; an authorized verifier checks it. Dispatch confirms vehicle arrangements and changes to loading deadlines or delivery promises. Accounts authorizes financial effects. The gate operator checks the current release and records actual departure. Staff-assisted and basic-phone operation remain supported, with the actual reporter and responsible recorder identified.

For a practical small-team arrangement, planning and dispatch may be performed by the same authorized person. The person who records a load must not verify that same load. A verifier may also perform the gate check if assigned both permissions. A person must not approve their own controlled exception or sensitive payment. Changes requiring an exception need a named responsible approver; an administrator permission does not create a general business override. Monetary thresholds and additional separation requirements remain under Q2 and Q5 until explicitly approved.
